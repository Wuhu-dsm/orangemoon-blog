<decision_coverage>
D-01: article has its own module/model while using shared title, slug, summary, coverImage, tags, status, timestamps, and soft delete.
D-02: article body persists editor-native JSON.
D-03: article schema excludes synchronized Markdown, HTML, and plain text body fields.
D-04: article category stays article-specific while tags remain shared.
D-05: article service generates and validates unique article slugs.
D-06: article schema and DTOs keep a summary field without implementing AI summary generation.
D-07: article cover/body images are URL references from upload output.
D-22: article status transitions support draft, published, and archived/unpublished semantics.
D-23: article delete is soft delete.
D-26: admin article writes require admin role.
D-27: public article reads expose published non-deleted data.
D-28: public and admin article routes are separate.
</decision_coverage>

<threat_model>
STRIDE register:
- T02-01 Information disclosure: drafts or soft-deleted articles leak publicly. Mitigation: service public methods must always filter `status: published` and `deletedAt: null` per D-22/D-23/D-27.
- T02-02 Elevation of privilege: non-admin writes articles. Mitigation: every admin controller route uses `@Roles('admin')` and no public write route exists per D-26/D-28.
- T02-03 Tampering: slug collisions overwrite routing identity. Mitigation: service-level generated slug collision suffixing plus unique article slug index per D-05.
</threat_model>
